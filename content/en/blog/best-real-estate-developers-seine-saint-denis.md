## Why does Seine-Saint-Denis appeal to new-build buyers in 2026?

### The most affordable prices in the inner suburbs

Seine-Saint-Denis shows an average price of around **4,169 euros per sqm** for apartments, making it the most affordable department in the inner Paris suburbs. The gap between municipalities is nonetheless considerable, which means thinking town by town rather than at department level.

June 2026 figures place **Drancy at 3,290 euros per sqm**, **Aubervilliers at 3,830 euros** and **Saint-Denis at 3,870 euros**. At the other end, **Montreuil reaches 5,690 euros** and **Pantin 5,610 euros**, two municipalities bordering Paris whose levels approach those of Val-de-Marne. The same budget therefore buys very different floor areas depending on the town.

### Saint-Denis Pleyel, the only station on the network connecting four lines

The Grand Paris Express is transforming the department's accessibility through its main hub. **Saint-Denis Pleyel** station opened on 24 June 2024, at the same time as the extension of Line 14 from Saint-Ouen to Saint-Denis.

> "Saint-Denis Pleyel station will be the only one on the Grand Paris Express to connect four metro lines (14, 15, 16 and 17). It will also be the busiest, with 250,000 passengers a day by 2030."
> Plaine Commune, plainecommune.fr, 2026

This configuration is unique across the whole network. It places the north of the department as a crossroads, whereas it was historically penalised by radial links towards Paris with no transverse connections.

### The Olympic Village legacy, 2,800 homes delivered

The conversion of the Paris 2024 village is one of the largest new-build housing operations in Île-de-France. Spread across **Saint-Denis, Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine and L'Île-Saint-Denis**, the site covers some fifty hectares and 83 buildings.

The scheme represents around **2,800 homes** intended to house close to **6,000 residents**, the first of whom arrived in autumn 2025. This large influx of new-build supply, accompanied by ground-floor shops and public facilities, durably alters the market structure across these three municipalities. To place the 93 area among other Île-de-France departments, a comparison with the [best real estate developers in Hauts-de-Seine](/en/blog/best-real-estate-developers-hauts-de-seine/) shows price levels almost twice as high to the west.

## Interconstruction

### The only developer in this ranking specialising exclusively in Île-de-France

Founded in 1960, Interconstruction is a developer-builder that has delivered more than **20,000 homes**, exclusively in Île-de-France. That is its distinctive feature in this ranking: the three other players run their operations from a national head office, whereas Interconstruction concentrates 65 years of experience on the Paris region alone.

In Seine-Saint-Denis, the developer markets **2 residences totalling 115 available apartments**, a volume clearly below that of the majors present in the department. Its positioning lies elsewhere, in its choice of municipalities: Aubervilliers and Drancy are among the most affordable in the 93 area.

### An offer centred on affordable ownership

The Le Fort program in Aubervilliers is eligible for the **reduced 5.5% VAT rate**, a scheme restricted to urban regeneration zones and the surroundings of priority neighbourhoods. On a budget of 169,000 euros, the gap with the 20% regime represents a substantial saving for a first-time buyer.

Both residences also include **notary fees** and are eligible for the zero-interest loan. The developer additionally publishes an analysis of the department's most promising municipalities on [its website](https://www.interconstruction.fr/actualites/ou-investir-dans-le-93-les-villes-les-plus-prometteuses-de-seine-saint-denis/).

**Weaknesses**: with 2 programs, choice remains limited against the 6 of Icade or VINCI Immobilier, and the twenty or so of Les Nouveaux Constructeurs. Its entry price, at 167,000 euros, sits above Icade's.

## Les Nouveaux Constructeurs

A national developer founded in 1972, Les Nouveaux Constructeurs claims more than **60,000 homes delivered** in France. In Seine-Saint-Denis, the player has the highest number of operations in this ranking, with **around twenty programs recorded**, spread notably across Aubervilliers, Stains, Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine and Noisy-le-Grand.

Recorded entry prices vary sharply by municipality, from **169,000 euros in Stains** to around 193,400 euros in Aubervilliers and 254,637 euros in Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine, a spread that directly reflects proximity to Paris and transport links.

**Strengths**: the widest choice in the department, coverage running from the working-class north to municipalities bordering Paris, and a clear positioning on first-time ownership.

**Weaknesses**: national steering that leaves less room for detailed knowledge of local constraints, and a price range that requires comparing program by program.

## Icade

The property subsidiary of the **Caisse des Dépôts**, Icade is a listed national player marketing **6 programs in Seine-Saint-Denis**, spread across as many municipalities: Saint-Ouen (Méridien and Mundo), Saint-Denis (Time), Pierrefitte-sur-Seine (Villa Lena), Le Blanc-Mesnil (Pavillon Morea) and Épinay-sur-Seine (Sepia).

It is the player going lowest on entry price, with **150,200 euros for Pavillon Morea** in Le Blanc-Mesnil and 150,600 euros for Sepia in Épinay-sur-Seine, two programs under the reduced 5.5% VAT rate offering studios to 5-room units. Conversely, Méridien in Saint-Ouen starts at 381,800 euros, which illustrates the breadth of its range.

**Strengths**: the lowest entry price in the ranking, the financial strength of a group backed by the Caisse des Dépôts, and reduced VAT on most programs.

**Weaknesses**: a national approach with little specificity to the 93 area, and a range sometimes geared more towards investors than owner-occupiers.

## VINCI Immobilier

VINCI Immobilier markets **6 programs totalling 93 available units** in Seine-Saint-Denis, spread across four municipalities: Noisy-le-Sec, Aubervilliers, Saint-Denis and Drancy, the latter alone concentrating three operations.

Entry prices range from **195,000 euros** for Élégance in Drancy to 278,000 euros for Les Terrasses Marceau, with 238,000 euros in Noisy-le-Sec and 250,000 euros in Aubervilliers. The player's distinctive feature is its timetable: all its deliveries fall between the first and fourth quarters of 2027.

**Strengths**: deliveries concentrated within a single year, which limits timetable uncertainty, and combinable tax schemes (reduced VAT, furnished rental status, Jeanbrun).

**Weaknesses**: a limited number of units, 93 in total, and an entry price 45,000 euros above Icade's.
